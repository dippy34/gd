# v8 — structure revision

Final file: v8.gmd. Internal level name: v8. Song: Xtrullor — Supernova (Newgrounds 593661). Duration approximately 4:20; the native editor rounds this to about 4:21.

All black holes, accretion effects, orbiting particles and their movement/rotation triggers are removed. The orange, pink and purple nebula, beveled obsidian faces and filled crystal spikes remain.

The route now uses 27 longer sections with 26 mode changes, down from 45. No three-mode sequence repeats. Grounded landings include raised bridges, cantilevers, stone platforms and overhead vaults. Flight sections use 88 architectural clusters with stepped channels, suspended shelves, floating islands, arches and larger offset rooms. The wave phrases change their height range and turn spacing instead of staying in one high/low pattern.

All seven classic modes are present; there is no swing. The drop at approximately 0:34 changes from normal speed to the faster speed, and later passages use the next faster speed. Music timing was based on spectral attack measurements of the cached song, using a 170 BPM grid with its first beat near 0.128 seconds. The level continues past the requested 3:20 passage to approximately 4:20.

## Verification

The final GMD was imported into Geometry Dash. A fresh native editor export contains all 48,518 objects in the same order with matching object IDs, and the decoration collision flags remain set. Native visual checks covered the raised bridges, block faces, crystal spikes and flight structures. No black hole objects or motion triggers remain. Original source files were preserved.

The independent export audit checks collision solids, hazard bounds, portals, speed changes, decoration flags and the ending. The approximate ground physics model passes all sections at nominal timing, with inputs 25 ms early/late, and with gravity varied by 3%. Five wave sections pass 331 nominal and individual-turn timing variants. These models do not establish a native completion or a rating. A full damage-enabled playthrough remains unverified; the level is exported as unverified.

48,518 total objects; 16,056 are marked high detail, leaving 32,462 in low detail mode. The song is referenced by ID and is not bundled in the GMD.
